# Steam Player Intelligence

## Business Context

Understanding how player engagement relates to player
satisfaction can help game publishers identify distinct
player profiles and better interpret review behavior.

## Research Questions

1. Is playtime at the time of review associated with
   recommendation probability?

2. Does this relationship differ across game genres?

3. Can Steam users be segmented according to their
   behavioral and review activity?

4. What characterizes each player segment?

## Data

Steam reviews (users & games)
